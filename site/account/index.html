Account guidance

Your account lives in the desktop app.

This public website does not provide a web login and cannot read your subscription or entitlement status.

Download VibeSpaceReview Access pricing

VibeSpace Access: $20/month after the introductory 30-day access trial. Optional AI/voice/cloud plans are billed separately.

Review or manage billing

  1. Open the VibeSpace desktop app.
  2. Use the verified account associated with your workspace.
  3. Open Settings, then Billing & Access.
  4. Refresh status there before starting checkout, retrying a payment, or assuming a cancellation completed.
  5. Use the billing-management action shown in the app to review renewal or cancellation options.

The desktop surface displays server-confirmed billing status. This static guide cannot confirm a trial, payment, cancellation, refund, or entitlement.

After checkout

Returned from checkout

Go back to VibeSpace and refresh Billing & Access. Access changes only after the service confirms billing state.

Checkout was canceled

No new subscription is confirmed by the cancel page. Return to the desktop app if you want to review or retry.

Need help?

Use the support route shown inside the desktop billing surface so the request can be tied safely to the correct account. For public software defects, you may use the GitHub issue tracker, but do not post secrets or personal information there.
