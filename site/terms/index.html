Access and separate feature billing

VibeSpace Access: $20/month after the introductory 30-day access trial. Optional AI/voice/cloud plans are billed separately.

  • The access trial starts on first use with a verified account, covers 30 consecutive days, and applies only to application access.
  • The trial does not collect payment, create a subscription, or auto-convert. Continued use after trial or grace requires a deliberate Access purchase.
  • Only an Access subscription you actually purchase renews monthly until canceled.
  • Taxes or processor-disclosed charges may apply where required and are shown in the billing flow.
  • Optional feature plans do not replace Access and may result in a second subscription.
  • Server-confirmed account and billing records control status; a return-page URL is not proof of entitlement.

Cancellation and refunds

To cancel a future Access renewal, use the VibeSpace desktop app: open Settings, then Billing & Access, and follow the billing-management route shown there. Cancel optional feature subscriptions separately.

Cancellation stops future renewal according to the status and effective date shown in the billing surface. It does not itself issue a refund. Refund eligibility is governed by applicable law and the terms presented in the payment-processor or account-linked support flow. A request or review does not guarantee approval.

If you believe a charge is incorrect, use the account-linked support route shown in the desktop billing surface. Do not post billing details in a public issue.
